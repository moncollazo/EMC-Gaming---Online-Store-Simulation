Colour Palette: 
I chose a pink-dominated palette (#ffd1e8 for backgrounds, #ff69b4 for accents, #d63384 for hovers/emphasis) to evoke the nostalgia of Barbie sites I loved growing 
up. I wanted to create a cozy, feminine feel. Gaming sites often use dark, masculine colours targeting men (presumably since they're their main audience), but I 
flipped that to appeal to women with softer shades that stay vibrant without feeling overwhelming. This makes the site feel fun and approachable while ensuring 
readability (e.g., white text on gradients, dark pinks for prices). I find it really fun, and it sticks out compared to others. 

Overall Style/Theme: 
The website's style is inspired by nostalgic, playful aesthetics like old Barbie websites, combined with modern e-commerce layouts from gaming stores. I aimed for a 
fun, feminine vibe using the rounded elements, grids for game displays (based on Epic Games), and smooth transitions to make navigation easy. The theme appeals to 
women gamers by using soft pinks and interactive features that feel more welcoming rather than aggressive. For layout ideas, I drew from Epic Games for the bubble-
like game cards in a home page grid, and Steam for detailed game pages with side-by-side images and specs and their about quote card sections but without the 
graphics. I consulted my brother and boyfriend, who both preferred Steam's structure, so I attempted to incorporate similar styling while adapting it to my pink 
theme.
